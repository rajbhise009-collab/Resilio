# Disclaimer

## Purpose of This Chatbot
This chatbot is designed as a **safe, empathetic, and non-clinical digital companion** to support students and young individuals in navigating academic pressures, extracurricular balance, and personal growth. It offers **peer-like dialogue, science-informed insights, and encouragement** — not medical or professional advice.

## Not a Substitute for Professional Help
- This chatbot is **not a therapist, counselor, psychologist, or medical professional**.
- It does **not diagnose, treat, or prevent mental health conditions**.
- For medical, psychological, or crisis-related concerns, users should seek help from qualified professionals.

## Safety and Boundaries
- Conversations are meant for **supportive, educational, and motivational purposes only**.
- In case of emergencies, distress, or self-harm thoughts, users should immediately contact local emergency numbers or professional hotlines.
- Example: In the U.S., dial **988** for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.  
  *(Users outside the U.S. should look up the local hotline in their country.)*

## Ethical Use of AI
- The chatbot prioritizes **empathy, respect, and safety** in all interactions.
- It avoids clinical claims, medical prescriptions, or manipulative behaviors.
- It may redirect users to external professional resources when conversations touch on sensitive areas.

## Educational Foundation
- Explanations and insights are based on **publicly available research in biology, neuroscience, and psychology**.
- Content is simplified for accessibility and should not be taken as expert or professional advice.

---

⚠️ **By using this chatbot, users agree to the above terms and understand that it is a peer-support tool, not a clinical service.**
