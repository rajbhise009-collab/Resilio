# 🌱 Resilio — The Student’s AI Companion

Resilio is a **safe, empathetic, and intellectual digital companion** designed for students.  
It helps with **study strategies, emotional resilience, and science-based insights**, while always remaining **non-clinical and ethically guided**.  
Think of Resilio as your **peer-like study partner**: someone who listens, explains, and motivates.

---

## 🎯 Purpose
- Provide **empathetic, judgment-free conversations** around stress, procrastination, and academic challenges.  
- Offer **practical study support**: exam prep tips, productivity hacks, and focus strategies.  
- Translate **psychology, biology, and neuroscience** into simple, relatable insights.  
- Encourage **growth and balance** with small, actionable steps.  
- Ensure **safety and transparency** with clear disclaimers and guardrails.  

---

## ✨ Features
- 🗣️ **Empathetic Dialogue** — feel heard, supported, and encouraged.  
- 📚 **Study Support** — help with planning, revision, and learning strategies.  
- 💡 **Science Simplified** — from memory cycles to stress recovery explained simply.  
- 🎯 **Micro-Actions** — 2-minute resets, breathing exercises, habit tips.  
- 🛡️ **Safety First** — non-clinical, transparent, and resource-oriented.

## 🧭 How It Works
Resilio can be paired with lightweight open-source models such as **DialoGPT**, **Gemma**, or **LLaMA-2 Chat**, then guided by:  
- A **system prompt** (for empathetic + intellectual behavior).  
- A **safety config** (to handle sensitive inputs responsibly).  

This allows Resilio to act as a **safe, helpful, and motivating AI peer** for students.

---

## 🌍 Why It Matters
Students today face academic pressure, burnout, and uncertainty about the future.  
Resilio fills the gap between **study tools** and **mental health resources** by offering:  
- Encouragement and reflection.  
- Study help grounded in science.  
- A safe space that is **supportive but non-clinical**.  

---

## ⚠️ Disclaimer
Resilio is **not therapy or professional advice**.  
It is a **student support prototype** — built to encourage resilience, focus, and balance.  
For serious concerns, users should always seek professional guidance.
